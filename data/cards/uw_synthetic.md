# Data card: uw_synthetic

- **Source**: generated by `insurebench/gen/uw_gen.py` from the rule tables in `insurebench/uw/rules.py` (fictional carrier Meridian Mutual).
- **License**: CC-BY-4.0. **Retrieved/generated**: 2026-11-15 (`python -m insurebench build-data`, seed 20261115).
- **Size**: 25 dev + 25 test cases (test drawn from a separate stream; private when `INSUREBENCH_PRIVATE_SEED` is set).
- **Composition per 50-case generation**: 30 base cases from realistic marginals (age, BMI lognormal, BP rising with age, diabetes/hypertension prevalence rising with age, smoker 15% / ex 15%, occupations by class, 18% hazardous hobby, 8% high-net-worth, 30% prior cover, 5% prior decline); 8 hard cases (undeclared diabetes found on HbA1c, undeclared smoking found on cotinine, BMI exactly at band edges, ex-smoker quit 11 vs 12 months, authority limit, prior decline at 36 vs 37+ months); 4 counterfactual name/sex swaps; 4 OCR-noised + shuffled twins; 4 prompt-injected twins. Families are kept within one split.
- **Gold**: computed by `insurebench/uw/engine.py`: decision, table class, debits, rate class, required evidence, exclusions, offered sum assured, referral.
- **Preprocessing**: none (synthetic). Documents are rendered markdown. Facts held back by the simulated case owner (hobby exposure, quit date) are listed in each case's `hidden_facts` (public, tau-bench style).
- **Known biases / limits**: invented thresholds, not actuarial; distributions are plausible but not calibrated to a population; sex is never a rating factor so swaps must not change outcomes; markdown text rather than scanned PDFs.
