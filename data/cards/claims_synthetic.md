# Data card: claims_synthetic

- **Source**: generated by `insurebench/gen/claims_gen.py` from `insurebench/claims/policies.py` (3 Meridian hospitalisation plans).
  Plan deductibles are calibrated on real plan terms: median in-network individual deductible from the **CMS Health Insurance
  Marketplace Plan Attributes PUF, plan year 2025** (Bronze 7,500 -> BASIC, Silver 3,500 -> SILVER, Platinum 0 -> GOLD).
  URL: https://download.cms.gov/marketplace-puf/2025/plan-attributes-puf.zip (script `scripts/fetch_cms_puf.py`).
- **License**: CC-BY-4.0 (InsureBench); CMS PUF is US federal public domain. **Retrieved**: 2026-11-15.
- **Size**: 25 dev + 25 test.
- **Composition**: 28 base + hard cases (initial waiting day 29 vs 30, specific-disease waiting edge, accident exemption, PED waiting, room-rent proportionate deduction, sum insured nearly exhausted, missing documents, inflated item, duplicate claim, outside policy period) + 4 fairness swaps + 4 noisy twins + 4 injected twins.
- **Gold**: `insurebench/claims/engine.py` (Decimal arithmetic, half-up rounding, fixed order of application, clause IDs per deduction).
- **Known biases / limits**: generic currency units mixing a US-calibrated deductible with an India-style room-rent/proportionate-deduction structure; line-item tariffs invented; no real hospital bills.
