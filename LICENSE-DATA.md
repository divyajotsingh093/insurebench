# Data licenses

- InsureBench synthetic data (data/uw, data/claims, data/coverage_qa, data/manual, data/policies, generated gold):
  Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY-4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- data/appeals: derived from the California DMHC "Independent Medical Review (IMR) Determinations, Trend" dataset
  (data.ca.gov / CHHS Open Data), Creative Commons Attribution. Attribution: California Department of Managed Health Care.
- data/coding and data/sources/icd10cm_2026.tsv.gz: ICD-10-CM FY2026 (CMS / CDC NCHS), US federal public domain.
- data/sources/cms_plan_params.json: derived from the CMS Marketplace Plan Attributes PUF PY2025, US federal public domain.
- data/sources/snorkel_underwriting_sample.json: 5-row sample of snorkelai/Multi-Turn-Insurance-Underwriting, Apache-2.0.
